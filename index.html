About

I work on making LLM outputs correct by construction. My current research runs an SMT solver inside the decoding loop of vLLM, so that fields a vision-language model extracts from documents stay consistent with each other: checksums, date rules, totals. Before that, I co-authored Speculate (ACM FSE 2026), which combines static analysis with LLMs to generate REST API specifications from source code.

I'm advised by Prof. Abhilash Jindal at IIT Delhi and have collaborated with Prof. Guowei Yang (University of Queensland). I'm looking for roles in LLM inference, applied AI engineering and research engineering.

Current research

Solver-constrained decoding for document extraction
2025 – present
vLLM · xGrammar · Z3 · Qwen2.5-VL
  • Enforces cross-field rules during beam search, not after it: GPU logits → grammar mask → solver filter.
  • Up to +16 F1 points over grammar-only decoding on passports, ID cards (MIDV-2020) and receipts (CORD).
  • 33% faster end to end by overlapping CPU-side solving with GPU inference; 3× faster solver filtering with incremental solving.
